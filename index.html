Privacy Policy

This policy explains how SocialSignal handles information when you use our platform to discover relevant social media conversations, understand opportunities, and prepare context-aware engagement drafts.

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Please read this policy carefully. By using SocialSignal, you acknowledge that you have read this policy. Where applicable law requires consent, we will seek consent separately.

1. Who We Are

SocialSignal is a social media growth and opportunity discovery platform. It is designed to help users identify relevant conversations, organize opportunities, and prepare AI-assisted comment drafts.

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at atharvbabar044@gmail.com .

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may process the following:

  • Account and contact information: information you provide when registering, contacting us, or using the service.
  • Connected social account information: account identifiers, usernames, profile details, permissions, and connection status made available through an authorized platform integration.
  • Social media content and metrics: eligible post text, links, publication details, and engagement metrics returned by a platform's API for features you use.
  • Product context: website URLs, product descriptions, target audiences, industries, topics, and other information you submit to help us assess relevance.
  • Generated content and activity: opportunity records, relevance scores, comment drafts, saved items, status changes, and actions you take within SocialSignal.
  • Technical information: diagnostic logs, timestamps, error details, and basic request information needed to operate, troubleshoot, and secure the service.

The information actually processed depends on which features are enabled and which permissions you authorize. We do not promise access to data that a social platform does not make available.

3. Social Media Integrations

If you connect a social account, SocialSignal uses the applicable platform's authorization flow and APIs to provide the features you request. The platform may require you to approve specific permissions.

Your use of Instagram, Facebook, and other third-party services is also governed by their own terms and privacy policies. Platform permissions and API restrictions can limit what SocialSignal can access or do.

SocialSignal does not claim access to every public post, conversation, profile visit, or engagement metric. Availability depends on the platform, API, account type, permissions, and applicable restrictions.

4. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Connect and manage authorized social accounts.
  • Retrieve available social content and account insights.
  • Identify and rank relevant social media opportunities.
  • Generate AI-assisted explanations and engagement drafts.
  • Save opportunities, preferences, and activity history.
  • Display analytics and service status where supported.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and secure the platform.
  • Respond to requests and meet legal obligations.

5. Artificial Intelligence and Website Analysis

Some features may use AI services to analyze product information, summarize content, assess relevance, or generate suggested text. When those features are used, relevant inputs may be sent to the AI service that processes the request.

Inputs may include product descriptions, publicly available or API-provided post text, and the instructions needed to generate a response. Avoid submitting sensitive personal information unless it is necessary and you are authorized to do so.

AI-generated content may be incomplete or inaccurate. Review suggestions before using them. SocialSignal does not guarantee engagement, leads, sales, or other business outcomes.

6. How Information May Be Shared

We may share relevant information with service providers that help operate SocialSignal, such as hosting, database, infrastructure, analytics, and AI-processing providers, where those services are used. They may process information on our behalf to provide their services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transfer, subject to applicable legal requirements.

We do not sell personal information for money. We do not share social account access tokens with other users.

7. Access Tokens and Account Security

Connecting a social account may require SocialSignal to handle an access token so it can make authorized API requests. Access tokens are sensitive credentials and should not be shared publicly.

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information handled by the service. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Do not use SocialSignal to submit information you are not authorized to provide.

Only the permissions you authorize and the functionality supported by the relevant platform should be used. You can request help disconnecting an account by contacting us.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account and opportunity records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal obligations.

Retention periods depend on the type of information and how the service is used. When information is no longer needed, we intend to delete it or de-identify it where appropriate, subject to legal, security, backup, and operational requirements.

9. Your Data and Deletion Requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your use of SocialSignal, subject to applicable law.

To submit a request, email atharvbabar044@gmail.com with the subject SocialSignal Data Deletion Request. Include enough information for us to identify the relevant account, but do not send passwords or access tokens.

We may ask for reasonable information to verify your request. We will process valid requests in accordance with applicable law. Some information may need to be retained where legally permitted or required.

Disconnecting a social account from SocialSignal may not revoke every permission directly granted to a platform application. You should also review the connected apps or business integrations settings on the relevant social platform to revoke access.

10. International Processing

SocialSignal and its service providers may process or store information in countries other than your own. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be applied to international transfers in accordance with applicable law.

11. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where parental consent is required. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us so we can review the request.
