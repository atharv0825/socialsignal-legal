SocialSignal Data Deletion Instructions

SocialSignal users may request deletion of personal information and connected-account data associated with their use of the service.

How to request deletion

  1. Email atharvbabar044@gmail.com .
  2. Use the subject line: SocialSignal Data Deletion Request.
  3. Identify the SocialSignal account or connected social account associated with your request. Do not send passwords or access tokens.

We may request reasonable information to verify your identity and locate the relevant records. We will review and process valid requests in accordance with applicable law.

Where deletion is completed, we will remove the applicable information from active systems as appropriate, subject to legal obligations, legitimate retention requirements, and backup limitations.

To disconnect SocialSignal from Facebook or Instagram, you may also need to remove the app from your connected apps or business integrations settings on the relevant platform.

For additional information, read our Privacy Policy.
